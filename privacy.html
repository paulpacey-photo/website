Privacy policy

Your data, handled with care.

In plain language: what I collect, why, and what you can ask of me.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Who I am

Paul Pacey is an independent photographer and consultant based in Prague, Czech Republic, working with international schools worldwide. For anything to do with your personal data, write to paul@paulpacey.photography.

What I collect

Only what you choose to send me: your name, school, role and work email; your messages; your discovery brief and any images you upload; and basic, anonymous website statistics.

Why I collect it

To reply to you, to give you access to my private portfolio, and to prepare for and deliver our work together. I never sell your data or use it for anything unrelated.

Who else sees it

Nobody, except the trusted services that run this website and my email, such as GitHub, which hosts the site, Wix, which handles the website’s forms, and Cloudflare, which provides anonymous visit statistics. They process data only on my behalf.

How long I keep it

Only as long as needed for the purpose you shared it for, and no longer than 24 months after our last contact, unless we work together and the law requires me to keep records.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how it’s used, at any time. Just email me. You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority (ÚOOÚ).

Cookies

This site doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies. Visit statistics come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying individual visitors.

Images on this website

Every image shown publicly on this website is used with the express consent of the school, or has been altered to remove identifying features, to protect the privacy and safety of the students pictured.

Photographs of students

Images from school shoots are covered by separate agreements with each school, which remains responsible for its students’ data and consent.
