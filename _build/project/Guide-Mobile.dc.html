Phase 1 of 3

Ready.

Align, agree and commit

01Ready.

We align on goals, scope and price

It starts with listening, before anything is planned.

  • Discovery brief: your school, in your words
  • Website audit: how closely your site reflects it
  • Discovery report: what we heard, and where to focus
  • A clear scope, goals and price
02Ready.

We lock in the dates and make it official

Once we agree on the plan, we commit to it together.

  • Dates agreed around your calendar and my availability
  • Contract signed
  • 50% deposit paid on signing
Phase 2 of 3

Set.

Design, gather, close the gaps, prepare

03Set.

I design your list of activities

Your goals, turned into real moments we can photograph.

  • Tailor-made activities, built around your pillars and goals
  • Options for every age group
  • Ready-to-send templates for teacher communications
  • A scheduling template to keep it simple
04Set.

Your teachers tell us what’s already happening

The best activities are often the ones already planned.

  • The school shares the activity list with teachers
  • Teachers flag what’s already in their plans
  • About one week for first responses
05Set.

We find the gaps and close them

Every goal and every age group, covered.

  • We cross-reference what we have against your goals
  • We invite select teachers to fill any gaps
  • About one week
06Set.

We finalize the schedule and prepare the school

Everyone knows what’s happening, and when.

  • Final schedule confirmed with every teacher
  • A reassuring, informative email to parents
  • The non-media student list checked and up to date
Phase 3 of 3

Shine.

Shoot, deliver, look back

07Shine.

We shoot, and stay flexible

The plan puts us in the right place. Then we follow what’s real.

  • One to three days on location (two is typical)
  • Room to seize unplanned opportunities
  • A school liaison: guide, second set of eyes and safeguarding support
  • Help with logistics and non-media students
08Shine.

I deliver your final edit

~300Per two-day shoot
72hOr as agreed
2 yrsOf campaign assets
  • A final edit sized to your shoot
  • My top images flagged for you
  • Enough assets to meet your media needs for two years
09Shine.

We look back together

A campaign isn’t finished at delivery.

  • A follow-up call to assess the campaign’s success
  • What worked, and what we’d refine next time
  • Your honest feedback
