FAQ

Quickanswers.

Short answers to questions school marketing teams often ask, each with a guide that goes deeper.

What photos does every international school website need?

Images that prove the four things every parent is looking for: safety, belonging, growth and joy. A warm welcome at the gate, real friendships between students, a teacher fully absorbed in a class, and students working hard at something they care about belong in every library. The Essential 10 shot list covers all ten, with what to capture and why each one works.

What makes a good school marketing photo?

Four simple questions. Does it look good? Does it feel good? Does it tell a story? Is it believable? The last one matters most: an image can be beautiful and still fall short if it looks staged. Test your eye with the interactive guide.

How often should a school refresh its marketing photos?

Every two to three years, or sooner after a new building, a rebrand or a change in leadership. A two-day campaign shoot typically delivers around 300 images, enough campaign-ready imagery for about two years of marketing.

How long does it take to plan a school photo shoot?

Allow three to four weeks. That covers a discovery brief, two short planning calls, and time for teachers to choose their activities about two weeks before the shoot. The planning guide shows every step, who does what, and roughly how long each takes.

Why do so many international school websites sound alike?

Because good schools genuinely share the same values, and they describe them honestly. In a study of 57 international school websites, 54 described themselves as a community and 49 used the word “excellence.” That isn’t a failure of marketing; it’s what happens in a mature market. Real difference shows in the specifics, and photography is one of the clearest places to show it.
